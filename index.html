Privacy Policy for BookShelf

Last updated: November 17, 2026

BookShelf ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to safeguarding your personal data and explains how our mobile application operates.

1. Information Collection and Storage

BookShelf is an offline-first e-reader application. We do not collect, transmit, store, track, or sell any personal data, accounts, credentials, or telemetry to external servers.

2. Storage and Document Permissions

The application requests access to documents selected explicitly by you (including .pdf, .epub, and .txt files) strictly for the purpose of opening and rendering books on your local device. All imported reading files, settings, and reading progress remain entirely on your local device storage.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

BookShelf does not integrate third-party tracking, profiling SDKs, or targeted advertising networks. Your reading activities and documents are completely private.

4. Data Retention and Control

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your information at all times. You can remove individual books, reset reading progress, or delete all app data directly through your device's application settings or by uninstalling the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is completely safe and private for users of all ages.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect application improvements. Any revisions will be published directly on this page.
